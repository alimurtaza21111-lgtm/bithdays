NAMAZ STEPS😊

1. Niyyah (Intention)

Before starting prayer, make the intention in your heart for the specific Salah you are performing.

2. Takbir (Raise Hands and Say Allahu Akbar)

Raise your hands to your ears and say “Allahu Akbar” to begin the prayer.

3. Qiyam (Standing & Recitation)

Stand straight and recite Surah Al-Fatiha and another portion of the Quran.

4. Ruku (Bowing)

Bend at the waist with hands on knees and say “Subhana Rabbiyal Adheem”.

5. Sujood (Prostration)

Prostrate on the ground and say “Subhana Rabbiyal A’la”.

6. Tashahhud (Sitting)

Sit and recite the Tashahhud, sending blessings on the Prophet ﷺ.

7. Salam (Ending the Prayer)

Turn your head to the right and left, saying “Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah” to end the prayer.
